## 🎯 **Query Patterns That Work Best**

### ✅ Good Query Patterns

- **Specific legal questions**: "What are grounds for X?"
- **Doctrine inquiries**: "What is the doctrine of Y?"
- **Procedural questions**: "When can Z be done?"
- **Comparative**: "What is the difference between A and B?"
- **Requirements**: "What are the requirements for X?"
- **Scope questions**: "What is the scope of Y?"
- **Case identifiers**: "SC_0001", "Case name"

### ❌ Patterns to Avoid

- Too vague: "Tell me about law"
- Personal legal advice: "Should I file a case?"
- Recent cases: "What happened in 2024?" (database is 1950-1966)
- Non-Indian law: "What is US constitutional law?"
- Fictional scenarios: "What if aliens sued?"

---

## 💡 **Tips for Better Results**

1. **Be specific**: Instead of "rights", ask "What are rights of arrested persons?"
2. **Use legal terminology**: The system understands legal concepts
3. **Try different modes**: Use Summarize for specific cases, Chat for concepts
4. **Use filters**: Advanced Search with outcome filters for targeted results
5. **Chain queries**: Start broad, then narrow with similar cases
6. **Check sources**: Always review the cited case excerpts

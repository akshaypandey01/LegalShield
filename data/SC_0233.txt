In this connection, it is also convenient here to notice the argument that the Committee is estopped by its conduct from challenging the enfor.ceability of the contract. The answer to the argument is that where a statute makes a specific provision that a body corporate has to act in a particular manner, and in no other, that provision of law being man- datory and not directory, has to be strictly followed. The statement of the law in paragraph 427 of the same volumP of Halsbury's Laws of England to the following effect settles the con- troversy against the appellants: "Result must not be ultra vires-A party cannot by representation, any more than by other means, raise against himself an estoppal so as to create a state of things which he is legally disabled from creating. Thus, a cor- porate or statutory body cannot be estopped from denying that it has entered into a contract which it was ultra vires for it to make. No corporate body can be bound by estoppal to do something beyond its powers, or to refrain from doing what it is its duty to do ......

The consideration which, however, appeals to us as one of overriding weight in refusing specific performance is that there are only a few months left before the unexpired portion of the lease will run out; indeed by the time the leases come to be executed in pursuance of the order of this court it would be scarcely worth while to carry on any quarrying operations. This, however, is far from being the case. Paragraph 20 of the draft leas' does not confer any right . upon the lessee to obtain a renewal· All that it says is that on the expiration of the period of the lease the lessee may, if it has duly observed all the fore- going conditions, have renewal of the lease on terms to be agreed upon by the Collector and the lessee sub- ject to the approval of the Commissioner. Assuming that this paragraph applies to the present case and does not require a lessee to have been in occupation for the whole period of twenty years, there are still two important conditions to be fulfilled before a renewal can be granted. The terms of renewal must be agreed upon by the Collector and the lessee. Where the Collector does Rot agree, no renewal will take place, and even if he does, the Commissioner may decline to approve the lease.

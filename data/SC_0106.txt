What does the phrase "in the course of the import of the goods into the territory of India" convey? The crucial words of the phrase are "import" and " in the course of". The term "import" signifies etymologically " to bring in ". To import goods into the territory of India therefore means to bring into the territory of India goods from abroad. The legal position vis-a-vis the import-sale can be summarized thus; (1) The course of import of goods starts at a point when the goods cross the customs barrier of the foreign country and ends at a point in the importing country after the goods cross the customs barrier ; (2) the sale which occasions the import is a sale in the course of import; (3) a purchase by an importer of goods when they are on the high seas by payment against shipping documents is also a purchase in the course of import and (4) a sale by an importer of goods, after the property in the goods passed to him either after the receipt of the documents of title against payment or otherwise, to a third party by a similar process is also a sale in the course of import.

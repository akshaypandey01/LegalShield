Basing themselves on one interpretation of the section they held the coven- ant to be valid and basing themselves on the validity of the coven- ant so found, they gave the section a second and a different inter- pretation. In deciding the validity of the covenant they had not said that s. 3 had no application where the covenant existed. If they had, they would have decided what they called the scope of the section without any aid from the covenant and there would have been no need for deciding the scope of the section again on the basis of the validity of the covenant. Therefore, on the second occasion thHY found the scope to be different from what they bad found it to be on the first occasion. But, of course, a section bas only one interpretation and one scope; a process resulting in more than one interpretation and scope is clearly erroneous.

In dealing with this question we cannot ignore the fact that the relevant provisions which fall to· be construed in the present appeal impose a serious penalty on the regis- tered dealer, and so, even if the view for which the appellant contends may perhaps be a possible view, we see no reason why the other view for which the first respondent contends and which appears to us to , be more reasonable should not be accepted.

The extent of the power of courts over valuations by valuers has been stated in text books and in certain decided cases. In Williston on Contracts, Vol. 3, s. 802, at p. 2252 the law is stated thus: " In the absence of fraud or mistake, the price fixed by agreed valuers is conclusive upon the parties.

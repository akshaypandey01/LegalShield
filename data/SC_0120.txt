The arbitration clause incorporated by s. 57(1) of the Electricity (Supply) Act, 1948, in a licence granted by the Government for the supply of electrical energy to the consumers is not· available for adjudicating upon a dispute between the licensee. and the consumer, for the licence is an engagement be- tween the licensee and the Government and the arbitration clause in it refers only to disputes between them. Section 57(1) does not make the arbitration clause a statutory provision by virtue of which disputes between any and every person may be referred to arbitration.
